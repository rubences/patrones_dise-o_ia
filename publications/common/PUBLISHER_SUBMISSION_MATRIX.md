# Publisher Submission Matrix

> Verified against public publisher guidance on **2026-09-10**. This file is operational guidance, not a substitute for the current proposal form or a publishing contract. Re-check every route immediately before submission.

## Decision rule

The two books are routed independently. The English research monograph is submitted first to the best-fitting international acquisitions route; the Spanish teaching manual is submitted independently to a Spanish technical/academic publisher. No submission describes one book as a translation of the other.

## A. Research monograph — English

### Priority A1 — Springer Nature, Computer Science

**Project:** *Architectural Design Patterns for Edge and Generative AI: A Pattern Language for Agentic, Distributed, Reliable and Secure AI Systems*.

**Verified route:** Springer Nature's public `Submit your Book Idea` path, which is used to match the idea with a publishing editor in the relevant discipline.

**What our dossier must foreground:**

- the scientific problem solved by a pattern language for AI-intensive systems;
- a clearly bounded academic/professional audience;
- competing and adjacent titles, explaining differentiation rather than claiming that no competition exists;
- fit with the Springer Nature Computer Science portfolio;
- author expertise and the research/evidence basis behind the proposed architecture;
- projected manuscript size, figures, tables and delivery schedule when requested by the editor.

**Do not assume:** that LNCS is automatically the correct series, that a sample chapter is mandatory at first contact, or that a historical ranking guarantees evaluation value. Series/imprint choice belongs to editorial fit and the current acquisitions process.

**Public sources:**
- https://support.springernature.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000083155-publishing-a-book-with-springer
- https://www.springernature.com/gp/researchers/campaigns/books/computer-science
- https://www-cm10rc.springernature.com/gp/researchers/the-researchers-source/publish-with-impact-blogpost/prepare-and-submit-your-book-proposal/27655466

### Priority A2 — CRC Press / Taylor & Francis

**Verified route:** contact the appropriate subject Commissioning Editor. Taylor & Francis states that proposals should be submitted to one editor at a time; editors may redirect internally if another list is a better fit.

**Publicly described process:** initial editor review → external peer review → final approval/board → publishing contract. The public FAQ advises allowing at least four weeks for proposal peer review.

**What our dossier must foreground:** USP, demand, author expertise, defined readership, competing books, detailed contents, research value and why the architecture is timely.

**Do not assume:** a fixed generic sample-chapter count. Taylor & Francis indicates that a subject-specific proposal form may be supplied by the relevant editor.

**Public sources:**
- https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/publish-your-book/submit-your-book-proposal/
- https://www.routledge.com/faqs/author-faqs/proposals

### Priority A3 — Wiley-IEEE Press

**Verified route:** IEEE Author Center provides separate proposal forms for professional books and textbooks. For this project, the professional-book route is the natural initial fit unless acquisitions advises otherwise.

**Publicly described decision factors:** need fulfilled by the book, market research and cost analysis, with consultation of experts in the field. IEEE states that completed proposal forms are sent to IEEE Press acquisitions via the contact published in the current proposal instructions.

**Why it is strategically plausible:** the portfolio explicitly covers electrical engineering and computer science, including systems science, robotics, sensors and vehicular technology—areas that intersect with the planned Edge–Mesh–Cloud and C-V2X case studies.

**Public sources:**
- https://books.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-book-proposal/
- https://books.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/submit-your-book-proposal/proposal-acceptance-criteria/
- https://books.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/

## B. University teaching manual — Spanish

### Priority B1 — RA-MA

**Project:** *Patrones de Diseño en Inteligencia Artificial: Manual Práctico y Laboratorios*.

RA-MA's public manuscript page is unusually precise and aligns strongly with this project: it states an interest in technical/professional texts, preferably framed within undergraduate and postgraduate academic programmes.

**Required public package:**

1. complete table of contents;
2. **one or two chapters**;
3. expected length;
4. expected completion time;
5. attachment submitted through the form as PDF, maximum 30 MB.

The current repository therefore prepares **two** sample modules, but the final RA-MA submission can contain either one or both depending on the strength and total size of the PDF.

**Public source:**
- https://www.ra-ma.es/manuscrito/

### Priority B2 — Ediciones Paraninfo

Paraninfo's University line publicly describes textbooks, professional guides and monographs for study and lifelong professional training. Its public materials also include Computer Science among its subject areas and maintain a specific author channel.

**Important difference from RA-MA:** in the public material reviewed on 2026-09-10, Paraninfo does not expose an equally precise initial package specifying an exact number of chapters. Therefore this repository must not invent one. Before sending the full dossier, confirm with the editorial contact what they currently want at first evaluation.

**Safe initial contact package:** concise project synopsis, target courses/audience, author profile, complete contents, estimated extent and schedule, plus an offer to send the two prepared sample modules immediately.

**Public sources:**
- https://www.paraninfo.es/universidad
- https://autores.paraninfo.es/
- https://recursos.paraninfo.es/descargas/TRIPTICO_QUIERES_SER_AUTOR.pdf

## Submission order

The recommended order is intentionally sequential inside each market, while the two markets can progress independently:

**International:** Springer Nature → if there is no fit or after a clear editorial decision, CRC/T&F → Wiley-IEEE Press. Do not create simultaneous duplicate submissions within the same publisher group where the publisher advises a single-editor route.

**Spain:** RA-MA first because its stated technical/university focus and public package match the manuscript immediately. Paraninfo remains a strong alternative, but its precise initial package should be confirmed with the editor before sending assumptions as requirements.

## Go / no-go gate before every submission

A submission is GO only when: the current publisher instructions have been rechecked; title/audience/USP are publisher-specific; sample material is the correct version; competing-title analysis is current; all figures/code have rights provenance; no contract with another publisher conflicts with the submission; and the two books still remain independently defensible in structure, prose, examples, figures and pedagogical/research contribution.
