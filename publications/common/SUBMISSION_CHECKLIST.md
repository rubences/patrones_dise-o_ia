# Pre-submission checklist

Use this checklist immediately before sending either publication to an editor.

## Publisher facts

- [ ] Re-open the publisher's current official proposal/submission page.
- [ ] Confirm the current contact route and proposal form.
- [ ] Confirm whether sample chapters are requested and how many.
- [ ] Confirm file-format and size limits.
- [ ] Update `publisher-submission-requirements.json` if anything changed.

## Proposal quality

- [ ] Title and subtitle are adapted to the target list.
- [ ] Audience is specific and commercially/academically plausible.
- [ ] USP is stated in one paragraph.
- [ ] At least five current competing/adjacent books have been reviewed.
- [ ] The proposal explains differentiation without disparaging competitors.
- [ ] Estimated length, figures/tables and delivery schedule are internally consistent.
- [ ] Author biography contains only current, verifiable affiliations and outputs.

## Samples

- [ ] Sample chapters match the current proposal outline.
- [ ] No invented benchmark percentages or unexecuted experimental results appear as facts.
- [ ] References are primary/official where possible and have been checked.
- [ ] Code samples run in a clean environment or are explicitly labelled pseudocode.
- [ ] Figures/tables have provenance and reuse rights recorded.

## Two-work separation

- [ ] The research monograph and teaching manual are not described as translations.
- [ ] Narrative prose is independently written.
- [ ] Case studies differ in purpose and treatment.
- [ ] Figure sets are independently designed except where reuse is explicitly licensed and justified.
- [ ] The Spanish manual retains its laboratories, exercises and rubrics as a primary contribution.
- [ ] The English monograph retains its research questions, validation boundaries and experimental programme as a primary contribution.

## Rights

- [ ] No existing agreement creates conflicting exclusivity.
- [ ] Open-source code rights remain clear.
- [ ] Companion website/repository rights are preserved or explicitly negotiated.
- [ ] Translation/adaptation rights are understood.
- [ ] Teaching reuse is understood.
- [ ] Repository/deposit/embargo terms are understood.
- [ ] Any ambiguous clause is marked `NEGOTIATE`, `BLOCK` or `COUNSEL_REVIEW` before signature.
