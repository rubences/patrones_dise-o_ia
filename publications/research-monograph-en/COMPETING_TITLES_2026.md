# Competing and Adjacent Titles — International Monograph

**Market scan date:** 2026-09-10  
**Purpose:** acquisitions positioning for *Architectural Design Patterns for Edge and Generative AI*.  
**Rule:** this is a dated market scan. Re-check titles, editions and publication status within 30 days of an actual proposal submission.

## Executive conclusion

The market is no longer empty. By 2026 Springer/Apress alone contains multiple books on agentic design patterns, LLM agents, enterprise GenAI architecture and agentic reliability. Therefore the proposed monograph cannot credibly differentiate itself merely by offering “AI design patterns”, agents, RAG or runnable code.

The defensible white space is the combination of:

1. a **pattern language at system-architecture level**, rather than a sequence of agent recipes;
2. explicit composition between probabilistic AI and deterministic control boundaries;
3. **dependability**: safety/security, resilience, semantic health, observability and recovery in the same architecture;
4. **distributed placement** across Edge–Mesh–Cloud, rather than cloud-only application construction;
5. high-dependability application analysis including vehicular/C-V2X scenarios;
6. a declared empirical programme with ablations, adversarial workloads and threats to validity;
7. an open 102-pattern technical substrate and evidence registry, while keeping the research narrative independent.

## Revised positioning statement

> This monograph does not introduce yet another collection of agent recipes. It develops a system-level pattern language for composing probabilistic AI with deterministic authority, evidence, resilience and distributed execution. Its distinctive contribution is to connect agentic control and MCP tool ecosystems with reliability/security patterns and Edge–Mesh–Cloud placement, then make those compositions empirically testable through explicit workloads, ablations and failure scenarios.

## Claims we must avoid in the proposal

- “First book on agentic design patterns.”
- “No competing titles exist.”
- “The only book combining AI and software architecture.”
- “Patterns are proven to improve performance” unless a specific experiment supports the statement.
- “MCP provides secure tool execution” as a general property.
- “Edge deployment improves latency/cost” without workload-specific measurements.

## Acquisitions implication

The strongest Springer-facing proposal should lead with Chapters 3, 7–10 and 12, not with basic descriptions of Pipeline, Router or RAG. The encyclopedic pattern catalogue already lives on the companion web. The book earns its place by explaining **composition, constraints, failure and evidence** at architecture level.
